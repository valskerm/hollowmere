# Hollowmere

A solo D&D 2024 adventure and valley town builder in a single `index.html`. No build step; your game saves in the browser.

## The campaign

Ten standalone stories, from level 1 to 20:

- **Levels 1–4:** The Hollow King
- **Levels 5–10, choose one:** The Lantern Fair, Iron Under the Hill, The Black Sail
- **Levels 11–16, choose one:** The Sky-Breaker, The Crimson Ledger, The Brass Tyrant
- **Levels 17–20, choose one:** The Ashen Crown, The Titan’s Cradle, The Unforgiven

You gain a level each time you finish a story quest. Every fight is built to the 2024 encounter budget for one hero at that level, from published stat blocks. Each story grows the town: quests unlock buildings and bring settlers, and each finale needs Hollowmere to have grown.

## Deploying on GitHub Pages

Upload `index.html`, `.nojekyll` and the `maps/` folder, then Settings → Pages → Deploy from branch → main / (root).

## Battle map backgrounds

Put background images in `maps/`, named as listed in `maps/README.md`. Missing images fall back to the plain grid.
